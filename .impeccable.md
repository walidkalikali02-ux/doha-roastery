## Design Context

### Users
Customers shopping for specialty coffee in Qatar, primarily using the storefront to browse products, place orders, and complete checkout. The interface should make product names, quantities, prices, and order details immediately understandable.

### Brand Personality
Clear, trustworthy, warm, and refined. Arabic copy should use accessible Modern Standard Arabic rather than dialect.

### Aesthetic Direction
An Arabic-first specialty coffee storefront with a warm, premium, calm visual language. Preserve the existing Doha Roastery identity and prioritize legibility over decorative complexity.

### Design Principles
- Arabic text and mixed Arabic/Latin content must remain readable and correctly ordered.
- Product quantities, prices, and order numbers must be unambiguous in RTL layouts.
- Use concise, natural Modern Standard Arabic for customer-facing copy.
- Keep customer-facing labels consistent across storefront, cart, checkout, and order status views.
